# Later cloth scene

Begin with a small flat cloth and a simple corner movement or one fold. Identify the required number of working arms and the input/control route before planning full garments.

The LeIsaac version inspected on 4 October 2026 supports keyboard control for a single SO-101; its stock dual-arm teleoperation launcher requires a bi-SO101 leader. Simulation-only bimanual work will require an additional suitable input/trajectory-control workflow, rather than assuming the stock keyboard option works for two arms.
